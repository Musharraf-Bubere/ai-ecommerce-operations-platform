# Evidence & Resolution Workflow Research

## 1. Purpose

This research investigates how sellers identify, report, support, and resolve e-commerce logistics discrepancies.

The primary scenario is:

A seller hands a shipment to a logistics partner, but the marketplace continues to show the shipment as awaiting pickup or in a pending handover state.

The objective is to understand:

- What evidence sellers may need.
- Where they obtain that evidence.
- How they raise support cases.
- How they track and follow up on cases.
- Which steps could potentially be simplified by an external application.

This research does not establish that every seller experiences these problems or follows identical procedures.

---

## 2. Investigation Workflow

The common workflow being investigated is:

1. A seller identifies an operational discrepancy.
2. The seller identifies the affected order or shipment.
3. The seller collects relevant evidence.
4. The seller raises a marketplace support request.
5. The marketplace investigates or escalates the issue.
6. The seller follows up.
7. The seller receives a response or resolution.
8. The seller records the outcome.

This is a generalized workflow. Individual platforms have different support processes and requirements.

## 3. Amazon — Missed Pickup Workflow

### Source

Amazon Seller Central discussion and guidance:

https://sellercentral.amazon.in/seller-forums/discussions/t/cc316f3b-10c3-4fbc-a2eb-29fd87e5a95e

Related seller discussion:

https://sellercentral.amazon.in/seller-forums/discussions/t/9bcaf774-0c06-4334-91fc-60d391641de0

### Documented Process

Amazon's seller guidance describes the following actions for an Easy Ship missed pickup:

1. Contact the pickup associate when contact details are available.
2. If pickup has not occurred by the specified cutoff, submit the Easy Ship Pickup Misses Issue Form.
3. Amazon investigates and may reschedule the pickup.
4. If the issue continues, create a written Seller Support case.
5. Include affected order IDs and relevant dates.
6. Retain the support case ID for future follow-up.

The guidance also describes the possibility of reversing affected seller performance metrics when the investigation confirms that the pickup was missed by Amazon.

### Potential Evidence and Information

- Order ID
- Scheduled pickup date
- Pickup window
- Affected shipment details
- Previous complaint or form submission
- Support case ID
- Relevant communication

The cited guidance establishes the reporting process. It does not establish that every case requires a signed manifest or a particular document.

### Product Opportunity

A future application could potentially help sellers:

- Identify affected shipments.
- Collect relevant order details.
- Record when a missed-pickup form was submitted.
- Store support case references.
- Track follow-up dates.
- Record the final response.

### Limitation

Amazon already provides a missed-pickup process.

A third-party application should complement that process rather than duplicate or claim to replace it.

## 4. Meesho — Delayed Scanning and Pickup Evidence

### Source

Publicly accessible Meesho-branded support training material:

https://sites.google.com/meesho.com/updated-alc/orders-tab

Important:

This source appears to be support training material, not a current public seller policy document. Its procedures and deadlines require verification.

### Findings

The material describes a delayed-scanning scenario:

- A seller reports that pickup has occurred.
- The order continues to appear in the Ready to Ship (RTS) state.
- The support process checks whether the seller has provided a signed manifest.
- If the manifest is available, the pickup date is checked.
- The case may be escalated to the operations support team.
- The material describes a one-day scanning-delay escalation path.

It also describes checking the order ID and confirming that the shipment belongs to the concerned seller.

### Potential Evidence and Information

- Order ID
- Seller identity
- Signed pickup manifest
- Pickup date
- Current supplier-panel status
- Support ticket reference

### Important Observation

A signed manifest may be relevant evidence for a delayed-scanning investigation.

However, the source does not establish that every Meesho seller must submit a signed manifest for every pickup complaint.

### Product Opportunity

A future application could potentially:

- Store seller-provided manifests.
- Associate each document with the relevant shipment.
- Record the reported pickup time.
- Compare the seller's report with available platform events.
- Prepare an evidence checklist.
- Record escalation and follow-up history.

### Limitation

The current applicability of this support procedure must be verified with current seller documentation or direct confirmation.

## 5. Flipkart — Failed Handover Support Workflow

### Source

Third-party logistics provider documentation:

https://help.eshopbox.com/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-raise-a-ticket-on-flipkart-for-failed-to-handover-orders

### Findings

The guide describes the following seller workflow:

1. Log in to the Flipkart seller portal.
2. Open the Help or My Help Center section.
3. Select a logistics-related query or complaint.
4. Choose the relevant failed-handover concern.
5. Provide the required shipment or order details.
6. Upload supporting order information when applicable.
7. Submit the seller support ticket.
8. Receive a ticket number.
9. Track the ticket through the seller portal.

### Potential Information

- Order ID
- Shipment details
- Failed-handover reason
- Relevant supporting information
- Ticket number
- Ticket status

### Product Opportunity

A future application could potentially help sellers:

- Organize multiple affected orders.
- Prepare shipment-specific information.
- Maintain a record of submitted tickets.
- Track ticket references.
- Record responses and outcomes.

### Limitation

This is a third-party provider's guide, not an official Flipkart policy source.

The current seller portal workflow and exact evidence requirements need confirmation.

## 6. Shopify — Shipping Evidence and Tracking

### Sources

Shopify shipping labels, pickup scheduling, manifests, and tracking:

https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders/shipping-labels/managing-labels/track-and-schedule

Shopify order tracking:

https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/setup/order-status-page/order-tracking

### Findings

Shopify documents capabilities for eligible shipping labels, including:

- Scheduling carrier pickups.
- Creating and printing shipping manifests.
- Tracking delivery status.
- Viewing shipment statuses such as picked up, in transit, and delivered.

Shopify also explains that a shipment with no tracking status may not yet have been scanned by the carrier.

### Potential Product Opportunity

For a Shopify merchant, an external application might organize:

- Fulfillment records.
- Tracking information.
- Pickup and shipping events.
- Shipping manifests.
- Carrier-related evidence.
- Investigation history.

### Limitation

Shopify's documentation describes its own shipping and tracking capabilities.

It does not establish that every carrier exposes pickup confirmation or that every merchant uses Shopify Shipping.

## 9. Potential Product Value

Existing marketplaces already provide support workflows.

Therefore, building another support ticketing interface alone would not establish a meaningful product advantage.

A more useful potential workflow is:

1. Identify an operational exception.
2. Collect the available platform and seller records.
3. Match supporting documents to the affected shipment.
4. Identify missing information.
5. Prepare a structured evidence package.
6. Help the seller draft a clear support-case explanation.
7. Let the seller review and submit through the official marketplace channel.
8. Record the ticket reference and submission date.
9. Track follow-ups.
10. Record the response and final outcome.

This is a potential product direction, not a validated feature specification.

## 10. AI vs Deterministic Responsibilities

### Deterministic Application Logic

- Match order and shipment identifiers.
- Check whether required information is present.
- Compare event timestamps.
- Detect missing event records.
- Identify overdue follow-ups.
- Associate documents with investigations.
- Track case status.
- Preserve source and submission history.

### Potential AI Responsibilities

- Summarize the investigation timeline.
- Extract relevant information from uploaded documents for review.
- Organize evidence into a readable summary.
- Draft a support-case explanation.
- Identify potentially missing evidence.
- Summarize marketplace responses.

### Human Authorization

The seller should remain responsible for:

- Confirming that submitted information is accurate.
- Reviewing extracted document information.
- Approving support-case content.
- Submitting complaints.
- Approving consequential actions.

AI-generated content must not be presented as original evidence.

## 11. Important Product Risks

### A. Existing Marketplace Tools

Marketplaces already provide forms, support channels, and ticket tracking.

The product must demonstrate additional value beyond simply reproducing those capabilities.

### B. Evidence Availability

A seller may not have a signed manifest or independent carrier confirmation.

The application must support incomplete investigations.

### C. Incorrect Evidence Association

A document may belong to another order or shipment.

The application must avoid silently attaching evidence to the wrong shipment.

### D. Platform-Specific Requirements

Different marketplaces may request different information.

A universal evidence checklist may be inaccurate.

### E. Time-Sensitive Procedures

Some support procedures may have reporting windows or deadlines.

These must be verified from current official sources before implementation.

### F. Privacy and Security

Order records, customer information, shipping labels, and uploaded documents may contain sensitive personal or business information.

Access controls, secure storage, retention rules, and data minimization will be important if the product is implemented.

## 12. Preliminary Findings

The research identifies several potentially useful operational activities:

- Collecting shipment-specific evidence.
- Maintaining an investigation timeline.
- Preparing structured support information.
- Recording ticket and case references.
- Tracking follow-ups.
- Preserving investigation outcomes.

The strongest potential value is not another shipment dashboard or ticketing interface.

It may be the organization and preparation of evidence across operational investigations.

However, this remains a hypothesis.

We have not established:

- How frequently sellers perform these tasks.
- How much time they spend on them.
- Whether existing seller tools are insufficient.
- Whether sellers would use or pay for an external application.
- Whether the proposed workflow can be integrated reliably.
